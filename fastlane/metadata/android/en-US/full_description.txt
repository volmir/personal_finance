Personal Finance & FIRE Audit is an offline calculator for a monthly budget, assets, debts and a possible path to financial independence and early retirement (FIRE). Enter monthly totals and current balances manually to review your financial position and explore assumptions.

FOUR SCREENS

• Inputs: income and expenses, liquid and financial assets, full property value, liabilities, nominal return, inflation, capital-gains tax, birth date and life-expectancy age. Expandable hints explain the categories. Choose whether financial assets count toward the emergency reserve.
• Metrics: cash flow, savings rate, emergency reserve, months of coverage, debt ratio, net worth, Fidelity age-based savings benchmark, financial freedom ratio (CFS) and real return.
• Scores & Audit: a total score from 0 to 10, component ratings and an automated assessment grouped into positive, neutral and negative indicators. Share the base summary or assessment through Android's share menu.
• FIRE: estimated retirement time and age, target capital and a capital trajectory chart through the chosen horizon. Three sliders and 14 scenarios adjust a separate simulation; saved base inputs remain intact.

MODEL AND SCENARIOS

FIRE uses a constant real return and the present value of future expenses. Starting capital is liquid plus financial assets minus debts not covered by property, with a zero floor. Property itself is excluded from investable capital. The model does not use the 4% rule or Trinity Study.

CFS includes property in net worth. Readiness to live on capital income is assessed separately on the FIRE tab. A neutral CFS can coexist with a green total score.

Scenarios include salary growth, market changes, expense reductions, inheritance, property sales, job loss and temporary mortgage/car/dependent costs. Job loss is a one-off stress expense equal to 12 current monthly incomes; ongoing income is preserved. Job-loss and loan amounts include the salary-increase scenario. Inheritance, sale limits and large one-off expenses use the original income. A sale is capped at the lower of property value and 20 monthly incomes. Uncovered one-off expenses become additional debt.

Capital-gains tax is estimated in a simplified way for the financial-assets share only when the initial financial-assets balance is positive. Other taxes, market-event ordering and income inflation are excluded. Results are guidance, not forecasts, guarantees or personalized financial, investment or tax advice.

LANGUAGES AND DATA

The interface follows the device language: English, Russian, German, French, Italian or Spanish. Choose among 13 currency symbols; amounts are not converted and no exchange rates are downloaded. Yen amounts use whole units.

The app works fully offline, requires no account and requests no internet permission. Inputs and simulator state are stored locally. Reports are shared only at your initiative through Android and the app you select.
